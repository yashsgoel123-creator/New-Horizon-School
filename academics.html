Pre-Primary · Nursery – UKG

Learning through play

Phonics, number sense, fine motor skills and first classroom routines, taught through story, song and structured play. Class size is capped at 22 with two adults in every room.

Primary · Class I – V

Building the foundation

English, Hindi, Mathematics, Environmental Studies and an introduction to Computer Science. Activity-based labs replace rote homework for science concepts wherever possible.

Middle School · Class VI – VIII

Widening the subjects

Science splits into Physics, Chemistry and Biology topics; Social Science, a third language option, and weekly coding/computersessions are introduced.

Secondary · Class IX – X

Board-aligned rigor

Full CBSE Class X syllabus with monthly mock tests, subject-wise remedial classes, and one-on-one career counselling sessions from Class IX onward.

Senior Secondary · Class XI – XII

Three streams, real specialisation

Science (PCM/PCB with optional Computer Science), Commerce (with/without Maths), and Humanities (History, Political Science, Economics, Psychology). Dedicated stream-wise labs and faculty.

Beyond the Timetable

Clubs, sport and the arts

Every student picks at least one co-curricular track each term — attendance is tracked the same way academic subjects are.

Sports & Athletics

Cricket, football, badminton, athletics and swimming, with inter-school fixtures every term and an annual sports day.

computer& Coding

Weekly labs from Class VI, culminating in an annual inter-house computerchallenge for Middle and Senior students.

Music, Art & Theatre

Classical and Western music, visual art, and an annual school production staged entirely by students.
