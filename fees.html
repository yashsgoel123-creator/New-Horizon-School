Clear fees, no hidden lines

Transport
₹18,000 – ₹28,000

Per year, based on distance zone from campus. GPS-tracked buses with attendant on every route.

Hostel (Class VI onward)
₹1,45,000

Per year, inclusive of boarding, meals and supervised evening study. Limited seats.

Sibling Concession
10% off tuition

Applied automatically to the second and subsequent child from the same family.

Merit Scholarship
Up to 25% off

For students scoring in the top 5% of their previous board or annual exam.

Uniform & Books
₹6,000 – ₹9,000

One-time cost in the year of admission; books alone are re-purchased each year at cost.

Exam Fee (IX–XII)
₹2,200 / year

Covers internal assessments; CBSE board exam fees are charged at the board's actual rate.
