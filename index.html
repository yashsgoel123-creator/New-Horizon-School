Why New Horizon

An education that stretches further than the syllabus

We hold two things equally true: children need structure to grow, and they need space to become themselves. Every timetable, classroom and playing field here is designed around that balance. Read our full story →

CBSE curriculum, taught deeply

Concept-first teaching from Pre-Primary to Class XII, with weekly assessment tracking so no child falls quietly behind.

Mentors, not just teachers

Beyond the classroom
