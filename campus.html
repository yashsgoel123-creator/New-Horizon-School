Campus & Facilities

14 acres, built one block at a time for how children learn

Dedicated blocks for the sciences, the arts, sport and quiet reading — plus the everyday infrastructure that keeps a school running smoothly.

Infrastructure

What's on campus, at a glance

36 smart classrooms

Interactive boards in every room from Class I upward, capped at 30 students per section.

3 science labs, 1 robotics lab

Separate Physics, Chemistry and Biology labs, plus a dedicated computer and robotics workshop.

Full-time nurse & counsellor

An on-campus medical room and a student counsellor available five days a week.

GPS-tracked bus fleet

22 buses covering the city and nearby villages, each with a trained attendant on board.

22,000-title library

A dedicated reading hour is built into every week's timetable up to Class VIII.

Boarding for Class VI+

Separate boys' and girls' hostels with resident wardens and supervised evening study.
