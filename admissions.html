Admissions 2026–27

Four steps from enquiry to enrolment

Admissions are open for Pre-Primary through Class XI, subject to seat availability in each section.

Submit enquiry

Fill the form on our contact page or call the admissions desk. We respond within one working day.

Campus visit

Tour the classrooms and labs, and meet the stage coordinator for your child's age group.

Assessment

A short, age-appropriate interaction — never a high-pressure exam — for Class I and above.

Confirm seat

Submit documents and the admission fee to secure the seat within 7 days of the offer.

Admissions calendar, 2026–27

Admissions open

Eligibility & Documents

What you'll need at registration

Age criteria

Nursery: 3+ years, LKG: 4+ years, UKG: 5+ years, Class I: 6+ years as on 31 March 2027. Subsequent classes follow age-appropriate norms.

Required documents

Birth certificate, Aadhaar card, address proof, passport-size photographs, and previous school's transfer certificate & report card (Class I onward).

Mid-year transfers

Considered where seats are available, subject to a transcript review and a brief interaction with the stage coordinator.
