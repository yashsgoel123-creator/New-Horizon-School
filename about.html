Our Story

Twenty-eight years of building the confidence to try

New Horizon School opened in 1998 with three classrooms and thirty students. Today we're a full CBSE campus from Pre-Primary to Class XII — still run on the same idea: children rise to whatever room you give them.

Our Mission

To give every student a foundation strong enough for board exams and broad enough for life beyond them — through rigorous academics, real mentorship, and the freedom to explore what they're good at.

Our Vision

A school where a child's report card is one measure among many — where curiosity, character, and the confidence to try new things are treated as seriously as the syllabus.

Since 1998

How we got here

1998

Founded with three classrooms

New Horizon School opens on a leased plot on Meerut Road with 30 students across Nursery to Class II.

2004

First Class X board batch

Our first cohort sits for CBSE Class X boards; the school moves to its present 14-acre campus.

2011

Senior Secondary wing opens

Classes XI–XII launch across Science, Commerce and Humanities streams, with dedicated labs.

2018

Smart classrooms & robotics lab

Every classroom is fitted with interactive boards; a dedicated robotics and coding lab opens for Middle School.

2025

2,150+ students, 98.6% pass rate

New Horizon marks its strongest Class XII result yet, with three students in the district merit list.
